## Data sources and dates

All figures are derived from **public, official** sources, each used as of the
date collected. Different layers carry different vintages, stated openly:

| Layer | Source | Vintage |
|---|---|---|
| Institution rankings and scores | NIRF (Ministry of Education) | 2025 |
| Per-institution scorecards | NIRF (Ministry of Education) | 2025 where published; otherwise the latest prior submission, as NIRF releases scorecards progressively |
| State total number of colleges | AISHE Report 2021-22 (Ministry of Education) | 2021-22 |
| District-level college counts | AISHE "List of all colleges" (data.gov.in) | 2012-13 |
| Accredited-college list | UGC (state-wise NAAC list) | as published |

Where a figure mixes layers (e.g. accreditation rate per district), the most
limiting vintage applies and is labelled in the interface.

## Accuracy and intended use

- Figures are produced by **automated parsing and name-based matching** of public
  documents. These methods can introduce errors; some institution names may be
  mismatched or counts approximate. Data is provided **"as is", without warranty**.
- This project is for **informational and educational purposes only**. It is **not**
  a substitute for official records and must **not** be used for admissions,
  accreditation, legal, financial, or compliance decisions. Always verify against
  the official source.
- "Invisible" / "unranked" means *not present in NIRF rankings*, which only score
  the top ~100 per category. It is **not** a judgement of an institution's quality.
- NIRF publishes detailed scorecards in stages after the rankings. Where an
  institution's 2025 scorecard was not yet available, its most recent prior
  submission is shown; each scorecard states the year of the data it is built from.
- District-level ranked counts are matched to districts by each institution's
  listed NIRF city. A district may therefore be undercounted, and a "desert"
  (none ranked) means none were matched there, not a verified zero.
